What's the problem?
Casual betting in physical spaces during the Super Bowl are chaotic and messy for people at the party. Squares games are fun in concept, but hard to run as the Super Bowl kicks off.
🧾

Confusing Setup Leads to Distrust

With physical gameboards, house rules being applied, all-cash transactions, and many participants, things can get messy very quickly. Disagreements over how the game is run sour what should be a fun experience.

💸

Payments Are Not Straightforward

Everyone depends on the host to calculate payments and transfer via Venmo. Apps like Splitwise have solved this problem for group trips the same logic applies here, but nobody has built it for squares.

⏳

Payouts Don't Come Right Away

It's been two weeks since the party and you still haven't been paid but you also forget to chase it. This gets awkward quickly, and often means winners just never collect.

Regulatory Risk Medium

Some states may classify this as gambling requiring additional licensing. Mitigation: Legal review per state, soft launch in permissive states first (Nevada, NJ, PA). Engage counsel pre-launch.

Adoption Risk Medium

Organisers prefer existing tools (Excel, WhatsApp). Mitigation: Zero-friction import from existing spreadsheet formats. Push notifications to existing Fanatics app users week before Super Bowl.
